UNCERTAINTY CONVENTIONS
Resolution half-widths 0.5 µm, 0.5°, 0.05°C, 0.005 mm are treated as
uniform rounding bounds and divided by sqrt(3) for 1-sigma propagation.
0.5 fringe is separately treated as an estimated 1-sigma count uncertainty.
x error bars include shared starting reading where appropriate; the fit
uses only independent x uncertainties because free intercepts absorb
a common additive baseline shift. Prism reference-angle systematic
is assessed separately and is NOT in formal n uncertainty.
Residual plots show ordinary vertical residuals; vertical error bars show
sqrt(sigma_N^2 + (dy/dx * sigma_x,independent)^2).
The chi-square of the two-axis fit measures both normalized x and y
corrections, NOT just the vertical residuals seen on the bottom panel.

LIMITATIONS / REPORT DISCUSSION
- No artificially added origin for green laser or prism.
- Only five green data points per run, fewer than manual recommendation.
- Missed fringes can occur randomly between trials but create cumulative,
  correlated step offsets within each trial; residuals may suggest them
  but cannot prove one was missed, or exactly how many.
- Mechanical jitter is a likely count-tracking problem, not additional
  measured micrometer-position uncertainty. Backlash not claimed when
  screw operated continuously in one direction.
- 134° prism reference selected once; unknown true perpendicular offset
  is not fully described by +/-0.5° scale-reading resolution alone.
- Aluminium temperature gradient / thermometer lag could explain
  different fringe counts at same recorded temperature. Not quantified.
- A few points may be missed whole fringes; do not discard them simply
  to improve fit; document any justified exclusion or alternative fit.
- Laser wavelength is shared across subsequent experiments, so its
  contribution cannot be treated as a new independent draw per point.
- The measured red LED wavelength, coherence length, and bandwidth
  cannot be computed without red LED observations.
- A small reduced chi² / R² mismatch is not automatically evidence of
  a bad plot; inspect assumptions, reference offsets and residual patterns.
